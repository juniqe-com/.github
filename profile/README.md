We're an online marketplace for art and design. Independent creators sell their designs with us, and we print them in premium quality on posters, canvases, apparel, home décor and gifts. Our own tech teams build the marketplace, the shop and the software behind it.

JUNIQE is part of the [ALL IN GROUP](https://www.all-in-group.com), a leading e-commerce group. Under one roof it brings together brands like MYPOSTER, JUNIQE, Kartenliebe and ArtPhotoLimited, plus our own production, Printhouse. What connects us: a shared mindset, real teamplay and a high bar for **really good quality**.
